Privacy Policy — SciTech Shorts

Last updated: August 2026

SciTech Shorts is a personal, single-operator application that generates short science/technology videos and publishes them to one TikTok account controlled by the app's developer. This policy explains what data the app touches and how.

What this app accesses on TikTok

Through TikTok's official Content Posting API and OAuth, the app requests only the minimum scopes needed to publish video posts to the developer's own connected TikTok account (e.g. video upload/publish permission and basic profile identification to confirm which account is connected). The app does not read, collect, or store data belonging to other TikTok users, and does not access followers, direct messages, or any other users' content.

What data is stored, and where

All data the app produces — generated video files, captions, source topic research, and posting history — is stored locally on the developer's own machine. No data is transmitted to, or stored on, any third-party server or database beyond the TikTok API calls necessary to publish a post.

Third-party services used

  • TikTok Content Posting API — to publish generated videos to the developer's own account.
  • Public news/discussion feeds (e.g. Hacker News, Reddit's public API) — read-only, to identify trending science/technology topics. No personal data is sent to these services beyond a standard HTTP request.

No advertising, no data sale

This app does not display ads, does not sell data, and does not share any data with third parties for marketing purposes. It has no other users besides its single developer/operator.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored data (videos, logs, OAuth tokens) can be deleted at any time by the developer by removing the relevant local files. Revoking the app's access from TikTok's account settings immediately invalidates its ability to post.
